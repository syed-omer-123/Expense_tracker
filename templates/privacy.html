Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Spendly is a personal expense tracker, so almost everything you put into it is information about your own money. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and what we do — and don't do — with it.

1. Data We Collect

We keep collection to what the service actually needs:

  • Account details — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense records — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Technical data — basic log information such as your browser type, approximate location, and the pages you request, recorded when you use the site.

We never ask for bank credentials, card numbers, or account numbers, and Spendly does not connect to your bank. Everything in your ledger is there because you typed it in.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to run the service for you — storing your expenses, signing you in, and generating the category breakdowns and monthly summaries you see in the app. We also use aggregated, non-identifying information to understand which features are used and to fix problems.

We do not sell your personal or financial data, and we do not use your expense records to target advertising. We will only share information where it is needed to operate the service, where you ask us to, or where we are required to by law.

3. Data Storage

Your information is stored in our application database, and passwords are stored only as salted hashes — never as plain text. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss and unauthorised access.

We keep your records for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual expenses at any time, and closing your account removes your personal data from the live service, subject to any short retention period needed for backups and legal obligations. No online service can promise perfect security, so please keep your own copies of anything you consider critical.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly relies on a small number of outside providers to operate — for example hosting and infrastructure providers that run the application and its database, and a web font service used to load the typefaces on these pages. These providers may process limited technical data, such as your IP address, on our behalf.

We ask providers to handle your data only as needed to deliver their service. Their own privacy practices apply to that processing, and we do not pass your expense records to advertising or data-broker services.

5. Your Choices

You can review and update your account details at any time, correct or delete expense entries, or close your account entirely. If you would like a copy of the data we hold about you, or want it erased, contact us and we will respond within a reasonable period.

6. Changes to This Policy

If our practices change, we will revise the "last updated" date above and, for significant changes, give notice within the app. Continuing to use Spendly after a change takes effect means you accept the updated policy.

7. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or about your data can be sent to privacy@spendly.example. Our terms of service are available on the Terms and Conditions page.
